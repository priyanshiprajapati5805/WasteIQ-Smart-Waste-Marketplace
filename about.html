OUR IDEA

Waste is not the end.
It can be a beginning.

WasteIQ is a B2B circular-economy marketplace that helps businesses connect reusable waste with the people and companies that need it as a resource.

🤝

For Everyone

Message, negotiate and make it easier to reuse resources instead of wasting them.

How WasteIQ works

  1. Supplier publishes a waste listing.
  2. Buyer searches and opens the listing.
  3. Buyer contacts the supplier or sends a purchase request.
  4. Both sides discuss the details using Messages.
  5. They arrange collection and reuse outside this demo.
